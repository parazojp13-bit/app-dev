## My Favorite Series: *Breaking Bad*

> "I am the one who knocks." - Walter White

**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama created by *Vince Gilligan*. It follows **Walter White**, a high school chemistry teacher who becomes a drug manufacturer after he is diagnosed with cancer.
